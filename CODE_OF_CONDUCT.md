# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in EngineeringDrawing.io a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive experience for everyone.

We expect all contributors, users, and community members to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Welcome constructive feedback.
- Support collaboration and knowledge sharing.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Help create a positive engineering community.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not tolerated:

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Offensive language
- Trolling or insulting others
- Spam or malicious content
- Publishing private information without permission

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Violations may result in:

- Warning
- Temporary suspension
- Permanent ban from the project

---
